# Voiceover: Necropolis demo video

For an AI voice / audio tool. The video is **110.0 s, 1080p60** (`necropolis-demo.mp4`; a copy with no music is `necropolis-picture-only.mp4`). Timings below are exact to the picture: every scene cuts on the beat of a 120 BPM grid, so one bar = 2.0 s.

Timings are also in [`voiceover.srt`](voiceover.srt), one cue per line. **That file is for timing the voice only. Do not burn it into the video as captions.**

## Brief (paste this to the AI)

- **Voice:** one narrator, low and warm, confident, a little wry: a trailer for a graveyard that pays out, not a hype ad. Clear diction, no vocal fry, no shouting.
- **Pace:** brisk, trailer-style: about 180 words a minute overall, with the montage lines (1:32–1:38) rapid-fire. Start each line at its start time. If a line runs long, speed it up slightly rather than letting it cross into the next cue.
- **Silence matters:** lines marked **[HIT]** have no voice. Let the drop, the crashes, the stamps and the heartbeat land on their own.
- **Mix:** voice on top. Music ducks 8–10 dB under the voice and comes back up between lines. Keep these effects above the music: the monitor beeps and flatline (0–6 s), the four REFUSED stamps (80.0–81.5 s), the eleven ticks (85.0–87.5 s), and the two heartbeats at the end (108.0, 108.8 s).
- **No captions or subtitles** on the video.
- **Pronounce:** Necropolis = *neh-KROP-oh-liss* · NEAR = "near" · MPC = "M-P-C" · EVM = "E-V-M" · $BONES = "bones" · COPIUM = "COPE-ee-um" · PHOENIX = "FEE-nix".
